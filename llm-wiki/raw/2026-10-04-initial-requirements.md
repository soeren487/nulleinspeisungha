Initial requirements as stated by Soeren on 2026-10-04 (verbatim).

I want to create an Home Assistant App for applying a zero feed-in strategy for my photovoltaik and battery devices. The requirements:

1. I have two houses with separate power connections. Both have their own PV inverters, batteries, and electricity measurement equipment at the residential electricity connection. So, the app shall support to handle several houses from one Home Assistant instance.
2. The PV inverters are all from Hoymiles and I have OpenDTU devices to read the inverter data and to control them. OpenDTU offers a REST API which shall be used. Hint: sometimes the OpenDTU devices need to be restarted since they do not get and set data anymore. This reset shall be automized. We have to develop a strategy how to recognize when one of the 3 OpenDTU devices gets stuck.
3. As mentioned there are three OpenDTU devices that connect in total to 12 Hoymiles inverters . I want to have the ability to globally connect to the OpenDTU devices and afterwards to select which inverter is used in which house.
4. Both houses have installed Shelly Pro 3EM devices to measure the current grid intake and feed-in. I want be able to add the measurement equipment per house.
5. I want be able to configure per house the power setpoint to limit the feed-in into the grid. The app shall than configure the inverters in that way that the feed-in should not get more than the given setpoint. Do some research about OpenDTU and the Hoymiles inverters how often the relative power setpoint can be manipulated and how much time the inverters need to apply the configuration set by the OpenDTU. The update interval shall also be configurable per house.
6. 4 inverters are having a DC battery in front that are directly connected to the PV panels. They do not deliver power till the batteries are full or in the night, when the time-frame for releasing the stored energy is there.
7. Each house further has a big, decentralized AC battery. The batteries are controlled and served by a Victron Multiplus II GX each. I activated to read the data via MQTT and I installed the virtual MQTT Grid meter so that I can provide the current power value measured by the Shelly power meters. Goal is that the batteries try to charge as much as possible by the PV power. But we also have Tibber as power provider and with that 15 minute dynamic electricity prices. In a Node-Red I implemented a charging strategy, that can be activated and deactivated, to charge the batteries from grid when the electricity from Tibber is rated as cheap or super cheap. In that case, the power value for the virtual MQTT grid meters of the Victron devices is manipulated by adding a negative value to the measured value of the Shelly devices, so that the batteries try to reach 100 percent charge till the next night is over. So, I also have a look onto the whole night, because sometimes the energy prices are cheap later than midnight. The maximum charger power is approx. 2100W per device. So, you also need to get the data from Tibbers Graph API per house. I want to move the feature from Node-Red to this Home Assistant App.

Let us start working on this by defining all the requirements in detail to plan the Home Assistant App carefully.
